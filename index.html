Design decisions

Car not charger - app connects via vehicle, not charger hardware
MPAN auto-filled - removes #1 abandonment point in current flow
Savings shown before consent - anchors motivation before friction
4 steps → 1 flow - vs. current fragmented website + app journey
2-week wait made transparent - progress tracker + est. live date
Pre-set schedule during wait - commitment mechanism, reduces drop-off
